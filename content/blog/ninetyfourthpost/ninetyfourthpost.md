                In real life, the Manufacturers' Trophy is replaced by the Manufacturers' Championship and the points scoring is different as previously, points were awarded to manufacturer depending on what points the top 2 finishing cars using that manufacturer's powertrain scored. Now, the top 2 finishing cars for each manufacturer are moved to their own grid and a scored accordingly. For example, if a manufacturer would sweep a podium (top 3 places), then manufacturer who would finish 4th scores 3rd place points and even if a car finished outside the top 10 but they ahead of at least 2 other cars that were highest 2 finishers for that manufacturer, then they would score points for their manufacturer even if they scored 0 points under overall classification. Bonus points for Pole position and fastest lap are not considered. Under the alternative format, eligibility rules for Nelson Piquet Jr Trophy change so that driver with one or less podiums (that podium must have come 'on the road' and no because of any subsequent penalty for a driver ahead) and without a Trophy championship.<br/>
