                    Seki has a board game out with the following rules:<br/>
                    <ul>
                    <li>Players have 2 items - water and gasoline and players start with 0 water and 3 gasoline.</li>
                    <li>Having gasoline allows the players to spin the spinner and advance squares equal to the number spun.</li>
                    <li>If a player passes a -1 gasoline square they lose 1 gasoline and if a player has 0 gasoline they cannot spin and can only advance 1 square at a time.</li>
                    <li>Water can be used to increase a spinner count by 1.</li>
                    <li>From the 3rd turn onwards, Killer players will begin playing from the start in their death car which has infinite gasoline and ignore square instructions.</li>
                    <li>If Killer players catch up to another player then that player is captured and is out (assumed).</li>
                    <li>The game ends when either all normal players are captured or when the first player arrives at the town at the end of the board.</li>
                    </ul>
                    The sole normal player that Seki has is making good progress ahead of the Killer players, but is ambush at the rock tunnel and is held back until Rumi fires a rubber at the Killer players on the rock tunnel, freeing the normal player. The normal player, however eventually runs out of gasoline and can only advance one square at which point the killer players have caught and about capture him but the normal player just about advances to the next square and gains 1 gasoline. The normal player spins a 10 and advances almost all the way to the finish and is 1 square short and Seki begrudgingly uses 1 water to arrive at the finish at Rumi's suggestion. However, Seki isn't satisfied and continues to move the Killer players, so Rumi is forced to deploy elastic bands to stop them but they have no effect. She therefore has to fire a mechanical pencil with the elastic bands, but she misses the car and instead strikes the wall next to seki instead which makes Seki stop playing and fear her for the next several days.
