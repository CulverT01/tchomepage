                    <div> 
                    For the Overall Drivers' Championship, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is after race 1 of Shanghai ePrix, the 11th race of the season. The leader at the point of the Mathematical ineligibility is Nick Cassidy, who leads with 148 points, but he odes not end up winning the title come season's end. That instead goes to Pascal Wehrlein, who wins the Overall Drivers' Championship with 202 points after Nick suffers terrible results in 3 of the final 4 races. Pascal was 2nd in the standings at the point of Mathematical ineligibility with 144 points.<br/>
                    For the Customer Trophy for Drivers Championship, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is after race 2 of the Berlin ePrix, the 10th race of the season. The leader at this point is Jake Dennis, who leads with 156 points. He goes on to win the Customer Trophy for Drivers with a round to spare and a final points total of 225.<br/>
                    For the Nelson Piquet Jr Trophy Championship, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is after race 1 of the Berlin ePrix, the 9th race of the season. Sacha Fenestraz is the leader of the NPJT standings with 151 points and goes on to win the Trophy with round to spare, with a total points score of 250.<br/>
                    For the Overall Teams' Championship, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is also after race 2 of the Berlin ePrix, with Jaguar TCS Racing the leader with 229 points. Jaguar goes on to win the championship with 355, sealing it with a round to spare.<br/>
                    For the Customer Teams' Championship, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is also after race 1 of the Berlin ePrix and the leader at this point is Andretti Formula E. They lead with 158 points and they go on to win the championship with 268 points, sealing it with a round to spare.<br/>
                    For the Manufacturers' Cup, the point of Mathematical ineligibility is also after race 2 of the Berlin ePrix, with Jaguar leading the standings on 256 points. Jaguar would go on to defend this lead and win with 407 points.
