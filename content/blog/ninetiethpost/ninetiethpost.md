    <h2 class="row">Qualifying</h2>
    <p class="col-8">
    Group A consisted of: Mitch Evans, Jake Dennis, Antonio Felix da Costa, Nico Mueller, Sebastien Buemi, Felipe Drugovich, Joel Eriksson, Jean-Eric Vergne, Taylor Barnard, and Norman Nato. Both Envision drivers have 3-place grid penalties for incidents last race and Felipe would pick 1 up as well after impeding Sebastien Buemi. The top 4 in group A and thus made it to the Duel stage were: Antonio, Mitch, Taylor, and Norman.<br/>
    Group B went next and consisted of Pascal Wehrlein, Oliver Rowland, Edoardo Mortara, Nick Cassidy, Nyck de Vries, Josep Maria 'Pepe' Marti, Dan Ticktum, Lucas Di Grassi, Max Guenther, and Zane Maloney. We had 2 drivers set identical lap times as Pepe and Pascal both set a 1:14.798 but as Pepe did it first, his time ranks higher than Pascal's. The 4 that made it into Duel stage from group B were: Edoardo, Nyck, Dan, and Oliver.<br/>
    The 1st Quarter-Final was between Taylor and Mitch, with the former winning by 0.555 seconds while the 2nd Quarter-Final was between Norman and Antonio with the former winning by 0.207 seconds. Quarter-Final 3 was between Dan and Nyck, which saw the latter advance by a 0.427 seconds, while the final Quarter-Final was between Oliver and Edoardo which saw the latter moving on by 1.207 seconds after a huge mistake for Oliver into turn 1 which is still quite wet because there are drainage issues in that part of the track. Semi-Final 1 was between Taylor and Antonio, which the former won by 0.127 seconds, despite both making a mistake in turn 1, while Semi-Final 2 was between Dan and Edoardo, with the latter winning by 0.16 seconds. The Final saw Edoardo winning by 0.677 seconds to become first non-British driver to score the pole position for a Tokyo ePrix race.
    </p>
