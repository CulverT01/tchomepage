                    Seki has got out a gold medal for the 2020 Tokyo Olympics which, at the time of publication, were scheduled to happen in 2020. <i>The fact he has a medal is impressive given the fact that the actual medal design would not be released until 24th July 2019 and the competition to design them wouldn't be announced until late December 2017 (this volume's Japanese publication was on the 23rd August 2016). Also all of the medals seen in this chapter have the wrong Roman numerals for the event as it should be XXXII (32) but the manga has it as XIIX (12 10).</i> This medal is designed like a folding fan so it folds up and is not the only design Seki has, as he has another design that differs depending what position its being awarded for. The gold medal has Mount Fuji and a wave (a scene seen on many a woodblock print), the silver medal has a hawk, and the bronze medal has a pair of aubergines and the significance of the 3 items is that if your new year's dream has all 3 in it (specifically in the order: Mount Fuji, hawk, aubergine) then you are guaranteed good fortune for the rest of the year. The 3rd and final design is based around mochi cakes (chewy rice cakes) with the 'gold' medal being a white mochi cake, 'silver' is red mochi cake and the 'bronze' medal is grill mesh to be used to grill your mochi cake medal before eating. Seki selects the mochi to be the winning design, although that is obviously not the design chosen as someone else is selected instead (in real life this is Junichi Kawanishi). <br/>Also below is an image of the actual medal design, specifically the gold medal
