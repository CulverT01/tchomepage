                    Seki is sick and therefore absent from school, much to the delight of Rumi who can now study in peace. Or she would, if she had remembered her Social Studies textbook, but luckily, Seki leaves his at school, so she borrows it instead. However, there's an issue with his textbook as the picture of Minamoto no Yoritomo (1147-199, first shogun in Japanese history to have de-facto rule of Japan) has an “X” next to it and the the flat staff he normally has is missing from the picture as well. Minamoto no Yoritomo is not the only historical figure to have their picture altered, as Takeda Shingen(1521-1573, famous samurai and military leader and was known as the "Tiger of Kai") is missing his fan and the reason is that Phantom Thief X (a character of Seki's own creation) has stolen both Minamoto no Yoritomo's staff and Takeda's fan. This ends up having an effect on history as without his fan, Takeda cannot block the blow from Uesugi Kenshin's (1530-1578, Takeda's chief military rival) sword and gains a facial injury in the process. Phantom Thief X isn't finished stealing yet as he steals: Amakusa Shiro's (1621-1638, Japanese Christian leader of the Shimabara Rebellion) ruff (white frilly collar which was fashionable in the 1600s) which allows him to fly, and Commodore Matthew Perry's (1794-1858, American Navy officer) fringed Epaulettes which means Phantom Thief X is the one that demands that Japan open its ports instead of Commodore Perry. Rumi is conflicted as one hand Phantom Thief X is evil by stealing other people's possessions, but because his actions have an effect on history is cool. However, Phantom Thief X meets his untimely end after being stabbed due to a case of mistaken identity. He had stolen the moustache of Itagaki Taisuke (1837-1919, Japanese politician who led the Freedom and People's Rights Movement and argued for parliamentary democracy in Japan) and thus suffers the same assassination attempt as Itagaki but unlike real life politician, Phantom Thief X dies. The lesson ends with no studying from Rumi, but this will be the only time today until Rumi realises that Phantom Thief X was reincarnated into this world as he has stolen the glasses of Taki Rentaro, whose portrait hangs in the Music classroom and she fails to focus on studying for the rest of the day.
